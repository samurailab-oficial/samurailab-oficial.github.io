Privacy Policy – Samurai Postagem

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Samurai Postagem is a personal tool used only by the owner of the Samurai Lab and Samurai GTA channels to upload his own videos to his own accounts.

What data is accessed

  • TikTok: basic profile info (open id, display name, avatar) to show which account is connected, and permission to publish videos to that account.
  • YouTube: permission to upload videos, thumbnails, subtitles and video details to the owner's channel.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to publish the owner's own videos. Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

Deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok or Google account settings; the local tokens are then deleted.

Use of TikTok and YouTube data also follows the TikTok Terms of Service, the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google API Services User Data Policy.
